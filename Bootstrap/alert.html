Essence of Varanasi

Discover the temples, ghats, and flavors of the world's oldest living city.

Spiritual

Kashi Vishwanath

Dedicated to Lord Shiva, this golden temple is the spiritual heart of Varanasi and one of the twelve Jyotirlingas.

Heritage

Dashashwamedh Ghat

The most spectacular riverfront in Varanasi, famous for its grand, deeply moving evening Ganga Aarti ceremony.

Culinary

Local Street Food

From morning Kachori Sabzi and Jalebi to the winter delicacy Malaiyyo, the city's food is deeply tied to its culture.
